# Data and rights notice

This repository contains original code, processed tables, derived feature
matrices, saved model outputs, audit records, and links or identifiers for
public blockchain evidence.

The upstream Trapdoor and non-malicious lists, blockchain event records,
explorer pages, and other third-party materials are not relicensed by this
repository. Users must follow the licenses, terms of use, attribution rules,
rate limits, and access policies of each upstream source. Derived files are
provided for research verification where redistribution is permitted; they do
not grant ownership of the upstream records.

The manual validation packet is a worklist and chain-level pool-state audit.
Event times in the released materials are reconstructed from pool-state
summaries and should be interpreted accordingly.

Before adding new raw downloads or explorer responses, record their source,
retrieval date, license or terms, and transformation step in the repository.
